## 2. Concepts

**A. The Launch Console (chosen).** Every protocol is a console: key slots (one per signer), the turn requirement (2 of 5), a timelock dial, a verified lamp, a last-change clock. A weakened protocol is a console whose required keys just dropped. Stands on refs 5, 9, 10, 14.
Hero frame: a warm-grey instrument panel in three-quarter view on a paper ground; five key slots, two keys turned; the TIMELOCK dial's needle resting against the 0 stop; one orange lamp lit beside "THRESHOLD LOWERED 3→2". Under it, large: **"Drift needed two keys to lose $285M."**

**B. Atlas of Control.** One object holding every control change ever, scrubbed by time (ref 1). Strong for the record, weak as a first fold (the reader must learn a legend first). Used for the record and the replay's time brush.

**C. Seismograph (v1/v2).** Rejected: control changes are discrete steps; drawing 3→2 as a tremor misrepresents the data. Used only as texture for the drain (ref 8).

Why A (judgement): the measured thing is already a physical idea everyone knows: how many people must turn a key, and how long they must wait. "2-of-5, no timelock" becomes legible in two seconds without a legend, and it is an object, which the founder's rule asks for ("the device comes from a real model… scroll-driven motion of one object is the part that reads as impressive").

## 3. Name and line
Name candidates (founder decides): **Keyholder** (recommended: says what we show), Two Keys, Quorum (exact but crowded).
Line: **"Count the keys."** First meaning: count the signers and the threshold. Second: the keys *count*; they decide whether your money is safe. Works on the landing, in alerts ("Drift: 2 keys now. Was 3."), on the badge, and as something people say to each other.
Tone: plain, numeric, calm; numbers before adjectives; never "hacked", "rug", "unsafe"; we say what changed and who signed.
Microcopy: alert `Drift · threshold 3→2 · timelock 0 · 14:02 UTC · signed by 2 of 5 · view change`; quiet `No control changes in the 30 protocols we watch since 06:00 UTC.`; empty wallet `No positions in protocols we watch yet.`; stale `Last read 4 min ago. The stream is behind; figures may be stale.`; unknown `A privileged instruction we cannot decode yet. Raw data shown. We do not guess.`; correction `Corrected 26 Sep 14:10 UTC: routine fee change, not a control change. Original kept below.`

Signal orange means exactly one thing: control got weaker.

Type: Geist and Geist Mono (Vercel, SIL OFL) for UI, numbers, addresses; Departure Mono (OFL) only on the console's small readouts. Labels: 11 px mono caps, like panel engraving. Grid: 12 columns, 8 px base. Feeds and tables dense; the console spacious.

How "threshold 3→2, timelock 0" looks:
```
 DRIFT · ADMIN                                        ● WEAKENED
 KEYS   [■][■][□][□][□]  2 of 5 required   (was 3)
 TIME   ◯──────────────  0 h               (none)
 CODE   verified ✕        drifted since slot 429,731,225
 LAST   14:02 UTC · 2 signers · durable nonce staged 23 Mar
```
Steps are drawn as steps, never curves. Every number carries its unit and source slot.

Motion: key turn 240 ms ease-out; lamp on in 90 ms, never blinks; dial needle 320 ms spring with one overshoot; counters roll once. Nothing loops. Scroll moves the hero console from three-quarter to front view once. Reduced motion swaps states instantly. Measured with the kit's rAF p95 check before showing.
