## The product's one job
Show who holds the keys to each Solana protocol, and warn before that control is used against users.

## The replay's one job
Prove the warning is real: in Drift's case Keyholder would have warned 5.6 days before $285M left. A first-time viewer must leave knowing three things: (1) two keys out of five controlled everything; (2) the alert fired on 25 March; (3) the money left 5.6 days later.
