# BRAND-TRUTH — Keyholder
- name: Keyholder
- purpose: A public, live record of who can move the money in every Solana protocol, and an alarm the moment that control weakens.
- core_verb: count (the keys required to move the money)
- metaphor: a quorum made visible — how many turns it takes, out of how many, and whether anything stands between the turn and the money. Not a key; the count.
- palette: ink #1B1A17 (primary form), ground #E6E2D9, panel #F4F1EA, one accent signal #FF5A1F (means only "control got weaker"); signal text #A32F06.
- constraints: reads at 16 px in a browser tab; works in one colour (ink on ground, ground on ink); SVG, ≤ ~20 path nodes; wordmark KEYHOLDER in Geist tracked caps; forbidden: padlock, shield, a literal key or crossed keys, hexagon/node, gold, purple, gradients, glow.
