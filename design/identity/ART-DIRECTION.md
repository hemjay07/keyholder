# ART-DIRECTION — Keyholder mark
**Rejected out loud:** a key, crossed keys, a padlock, a shield, a keyhole. Every crypto-security product owns those; they say "safe" when our product says "count".

**Signature element — the quorum row.** A row of five keyswitch slots, drawn as short rounded bars inside one rounded plate. An upright bar is a slot waiting; a bar turned 90° (horizontal) is a key turned. The mark shows **two turned of five** — Drift's council, the product's first sentence — and one small signal-orange dot at the right end of the plate: the lamp. Read left to right it is a sentence: two keys, three waiting, lamp lit. It is the console, reduced to the row the product exists to count.

**Favicon (16/32):** the plate with three slots — two turned, one upright — and the lamp dot. At 16 px: 3 bars + 1 dot, 2 px strokes.

**Material language:** flat engraved-panel graphics, like printed labels on the console face: solid ink shapes, rounded 2 px corners, no gradients, no shadows. In share cards the 3D console carries material; the mark stays flat.

**Composition:** plate 5:2, centred, generous margin; wordmark KEYHOLDER in Geist caps, tracking 0.14 em, to the right of the plate at cap-height = plate height × 0.5.

**Palette:** ink on ground (light), ground on ink (dark); the lamp is the only colour (#FF5A1F).

**Dual legibility:** at 16 px "two bars across, one upright, orange dot"; at 512 px the slot ends read as machined, rounded, the plate's inset hairline visible.

**Forbidden:** key shapes, keyholes, padlock, shield, hexagon/node, sparkles, glow, gradient, gold, purple, any text inside the mark.
