# Content, videos and demos: plan (2026-10-09)

From a five-way brainstorm (pragmatist, radical, analogist, audience, contrarian) and a synthesis, checked against the record.
Corrections to the synthesis: the Drift replay shows Keyholder's **alert rules** firing 5.6 days early (require_stage did not exist
then); no block numbers are invented: every figure comes from the replay data, the Control Record, or devnet transactions.

Rules every piece obeys: each figure carries its record (day, transaction, or API path); no protocol is called reckless or
owned beyond what the chain proves ("built from", "controlled by a 4-of-10 multisig"); films are music only, no voice; every
post carries an image and the text is its caption; no depositor is made to feel foolish.
